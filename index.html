Built for bars that rehire every season

Cape Cod bars rebuild most of their staff every spring. Managers spend opening weeks re-explaining the same specs to a new crew, and inconsistent pours cost money all summer.

PourPrep is for owners and managers of seasonal and high-turnover bars — beach towns, college towns, and busy restaurants — who need new staff trained fast and consistently without pulling a manager off the floor.
